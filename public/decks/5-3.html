Technical texts

Place

above, beneath, adjacent to

Tells you the label's position.

Sequence

subsequently, prior to, then

Tells you the order of steps.

Purpose

in order to, so that, which enables

Tells you the function.

You do not need to understand every technical word. Focus on what it does and where it is.

Reading5 / 8

The Future Perfect

Future Simple

I will finish the course in May.

A future event

Future Perfect

By June, I will have finished the course.

Complete before a future time

Continuous

By May, I will have been studying for a year.

Duration up to a future time

Form: will have + past participle. Signals: by, by then, by the time.

Grammar6 / 8

Common errors

Missing have

✗By 2030, it will finished.

✓By 2030, it will have finished.

Wrong tense with by

✗By June, I finish.

✓By June, I will have finished.

Time clause

✗By the time you will come, I'll have left.

✓By the time you come, I'll have left.
