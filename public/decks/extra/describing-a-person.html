Describing a person2 / 15

A good description has four parts

1Who they areMy grandfather is the person I admire most.
2What they look likeHe is tall, with grey hair and a warm smile.
3What they are likeHe is patient and always ready to listen.
4Why they matterHe taught me that hard work pays off.

Keep appearance short. Examiners care more about personality and your reasons.

Personality8 / 15

Adjective + example

B1

She is generous.

An adjective alone

B2

She is generous. She always pays for the coffee, even when she is short of money.

A short example proves it

B2+

She is the kind of person who always pays for the coffee, even when she is short of money.

One smooth sentence

One adjective with one example is stronger than five adjectives in a row.

Relationships10 / 15

Three structures to sound natural

1

He is the kind of person who never gives up.

Use who for people, not which.

2

She is someone I can always rely on.

Short and very common in speaking.

3

What I admire most about him is his patience.

Good for your reason in Part 2.

Use each structure once. Repeating the same one sounds memorised.

Describing a person11 / 15

Speaking Part 2

Describe a person you admire.

You should say:
who this person is
how you know them
what they are like
and explain why you admire them.

Your two-minute plan

15 sec who they are
20 sec what they look like
45 sec personality + one example
40 sec why you admire them

You have one minute to prepare. Write only keywords, not sentences.

Speaking12 / 15

Model answer

I'd like to talk about my grandmother, who is the person I admire most. She is in her early seventies, quite short, with silver hair and a warm smile. What I admire most about her is her determination. She raised four children on her own and still found time to help the neighbours. When I failed my first exam, she told me that mistakes are part of learning, so I should keep going. She is the kind of person who never complains. I really look up to her, and I hope I take after her.

Appearance takes one sentence. The rest is personality, a real example and the reason.

Speaking13 / 15

Common mistakes

✗He has 30 years.

✓He is 30 years old.

✗Her hairs are black.

✓Her hair is black.

✗She is very sympathetic.

✓She is very good-looking.

Sympathetic means understanding, not attractive.

✗He looks as his father.

✓He looks like his father.

Your checklist

1Say who the person is and how you know them
2Give two or three details about appearance
3Choose three personality adjectives, not ten
4Add a short example for each adjective
5Soften negative words: a bit, tends to, rather
6Finish with why this person matters to you
