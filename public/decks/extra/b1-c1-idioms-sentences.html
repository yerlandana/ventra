One idea, three levels

B1

Many people use phones at night. It is bad for sleep.

Two short sentences, basic words

B2

Many people use their phones before bed, which can damage their sleep.

One sentence joined with a relative clause

C1

Scrolling late into the night, many people are unaware of how much their sleep suffers.

Participle clause and a noun clause, precise verbs

C1 is not longer words. It is more control over how ideas connect.

How to use idioms without sounding learned

1One or two per answer, never a list
2Only where the meaning fits exactly
3Extend it: give a reason or example
4Skip old clichés like "raining cats and dogs"

Absolutely, I've always had itchy feet. I'd much rather go somewhere off the beaten track than a resort, because that's where you find hidden gems.

Speaking10 / 20

Listening: wait for the end of the sentence

1
Contrast

but actually · although · however

2
Correction

sorry, I mean · no, wait · or rather

3
Final choice

in the end · so we went for · we settled on

"We were going to meet on Tuesday, but actually Thursday suits everyone better."

The first day you hear is the distractor. The structure after "but actually" carries the answer.

Reading14 / 20

Reading: the same idea, a new sentence

1Active → passiveResearchers tested 200 volunteers.200 volunteers were tested.
2Verb → nounPrices increased sharply.a sharp increase in prices
3Clause → phrasebecause the weather was baddue to poor weather
4Double negativeThis is not uncommon.This happens fairly often.

The question rarely copies the text. Look for the same meaning in a different structure.

Reading: True, False or Not Given

"Although the bridge was completed in 1932, it was not opened to the public until three years later, when safety tests had finally been passed."

1The bridge opened to the public in 1932.FALSE
2Safety checks delayed the opening.TRUE
3It was the longest bridge in the country.NOT GIVEN

"not … until three years later" = it opened in 1935, and "when tests had been passed" explains the delay.

Writing: build one sentence

B1

Cities are growing. Traffic is getting worse.

Two simple sentences

B2

As cities grow, traffic gets worse.

Linked with a time conjunction

B2+

As cities grow, congestion worsens, which costs businesses time and money.

Result added as a relative clause

C1

With urban populations rising rapidly, congestion has become a serious economic burden.

"With + noun + -ing" and a noun phrase

Writing: five C1 structures

1Participle clauseHaving considered both views, I believe the benefits outweigh the costs.
2InversionNot only does tourism create jobs, but it also funds local services.
3Cleft sentenceWhat worries many parents is the amount of time children spend online.

Writing18 / 20

Writing: fix it, then link it

Comma splice

✗Technology is useful, it saves time.

✓Technology is useful because it saves time.

Fragment

✗Because many people work from home.

✓Because many people work from home, offices are emptier.

Agreement

✗There is many reasons for this.

✓There are many reasons for this.

Link sentences with This / Such + noun

Many young people now work remotely. This trend has changed how cities plan their offices.
